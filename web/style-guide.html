Typography

Display

Anton

Headlines only. Single weight (400) — never bold it, synthetic bolding wrecks the condensed letterforms. Always uppercase, always tight leading. Get hierarchy from size and colour, never from weight.

--font-display

Body

Montserrat

Everything that isn't a headline. Weights 300–700 loaded. 400 for prose, 500–600 for labels and emphasis, 700 for eyebrows only. The live site also serves Roboto and Open Sans as platform defaults — ignore both, Montserrat is the intentional choice.

Rules

Do keep dark as the default surface. Black is the brand's native ground.
Do let cyan carry brand identity and structural emphasis only.
Do set every number that matters in Anton — it is the loudest thing available.
Don't bold Anton, or set it in sentence case.
Don't use cyan to mean "good". Green means good; cyan means Primal.
Don't introduce a colour that isn't on this page.
Don't put long prose in Anton. It is a display face and falls apart past a few words.

Provenance

Audited 2026-07-31. The live page resolves to a Leadpages-hosted application funnel, which serves several fonts the design does not actually use (Roboto, Open Sans, Rethink Sans, Inter).
